Help & contact

A question, a bug, an idea for a discovery? Write to us: ambinina@gmail.com. We read every message.

Manage or cancel my subscription

On your iPhone: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > DING. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

Get my subscription back on a new iPhone

In DING: ⋯ > Settings > Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

Request a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

My progress disappeared

Make sure iCloud is on with the same Apple ID, then reopen DING: your constellation syncs back.
