Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

DING is designed to respect your privacy: the app collects no personal data, shows no ads and does not track you across apps.

What stays on your device

Your progress (completed discoveries, constellation, favorites, sound and reminder preferences) is stored on your iPhone. It is never sent to any DING server.

iCloud sync

If iCloud is enabled on your device, this progress may be synced across your devices through your iCloud account. This data is stored by Apple under its own privacy policy; DING cannot access it outside your app.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. DING never receives your name, email address or payment details: the app only knows whether a subscription is active.

Notifications

Daily reminders are optional and scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off at any time in DING or in iOS Settings.

Analytics

DING includes no third-party analytics or tracking tools. Apple may provide the developer with aggregated, anonymous statistics (downloads, crash reports) if you agreed to share them in your iPhone settings.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app erases the data stored on your device. To delete the iCloud copy: Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Children

DING collects data from no one, including children.
